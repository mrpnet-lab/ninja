CONTEXT — WHAT Mm_ATM_v3.cs IS
--------------------------------
Mm_ATM_v3.cs is a NQ automated trading strategy evolved from Mm_ATM.cs.
The v1 (Mm_ATM.cs) traded frequently and performed well. v3 almost never fires auto
entries in testing. The root cause analysis (in Mm_Auto_Strategy_comp_enhancements.txt)
has identified the following confirmed problems:

  PROBLEM 1 — DOUBLE LUNCH PENALTY
    ApplySmartFilters applies 0.85x multiplier for 11:30-14:00 (Filter 14).
    Then CalculateSignals subtracts another 10 raw points after ApplySmartFilters.
    The 45% floor in ApplySmartFilters fires before the -10, so the floor is bypassed.
    Result: a score of 36 (at floor) becomes 26 after the -10, which is under the
    55-point threshold. The lunch penalty is applied twice.

  PROBLEM 2 — HTF FILTER TOO TIGHT (0.5 ATR threshold)
    Filter 6: if |price - htfEma| > 0.5 * ATR -> 0.6x penalty (40% cut).
    NQ intraday price is routinely 2-5 ATR from the HTF EMA during trend days.
    This threshold fires on almost every bar of a trending NQ day, killing
    all counter-trend signals even when a clear short-term reversal is evident.
    Should be 1.5 ATR (meaningful structural deviation from HTF, not noise).

  PROBLEM 3 — DUPLICATE POC FILTERS (Filters 10 and 13)
    Filter 10 (near POC <0.3 ATR): 0.85x penalty.
    Filter 13 (POC Magnetic <1.0 ATR): 0.75x penalty.
    When Filter 10 fires, Filter 13 almost always also fires.
    Combined: 0.85 x 0.75 = 0.64x — a 36% reduction near POC.
    POC is the most-traded price level (institutional magnet). Aggressive
    penalization near POC eliminates entries at exactly the highest-volume zone.

  PROBLEM 4 — AUTO-SELECT STRATEGY SELECTION IS WRONG
    v3 CalculateSignals (autoStrategy == 4) calculates raw scores for all 4
    strategies, then picks the winner by raw score, THEN applies ApplySmartFilters
    once. The winner by raw score may not be the winner after filtering.
    v1 applied filters per strategy individually, then picked filtered best.
    v3 also adds a 3-bar stability lock (require same best strategy 3 bars).
    Combined with session overrides (force LSR 9:30-10:00, force Momentum 11:30-14:00),
    the stability filter delays valid entries and the session override blocks
    momentum-trending mornings (the best trade setups of the day).

  PROBLEM 5 — SMART TRAIL: SPIKE WIDENING WHEN SHOULD TIGHTEN
    CalculateTrailDistance: if (atr > atrAvg * TRAIL_SPIKE_MULT) spikeMult = 1.5.
    During volatility spikes (when protecting gains matters most), the trail
    WIDENS by 50%. This is backward — spikes = tighten to lock profits.

  PROBLEM 6 — SMART TRAIL SPIKE GUARD FREEZES TRAIL
    MonitorAdaptiveTrail: if (barRange > atr * 3.0) return early.
    During the most volatile bars (gap opens, news spikes), the trail is frozen.
    If price spikes against the position during one of these bars, hiddenSL
    is also not updated, leading to unprotected exposure.

  PROBLEM 7 — SMART TRAIL PAUSE FIRES TOO RARELY
    Pause requires trailMaxProfitPts >= dynamicActivation * 2.
    With dynamicActivation = max(8, atrPts * 0.4):
    - NQ ATR=25pts -> dynActivation=10 -> need 20 pts max profit before pause eligible.
    For tickets that reach T1-BE (15 pts) but not T2 (25 pts), S-TRAIL never pauses.
    Change threshold to dynamicActivation * 1.5 to allow pause at T1-BE level.

  PROBLEM 8 — SMART SL MODE C : BUG IN EXTENSION FORMULA
    Code:   double extendPts = Math.Min(10, slPoints + 30 - slPoints);
            // slPoints + 30 - slPoints = 30 always -> Math.Min(10, 30) = 10
    Always extends by exactly 10 pts regardless of slPoints. Likely intended
    to be proportional to SL distance.

  PROBLEM 9 — S-TRAIL RESUME LOG BUG
    smartTrailPauseBars is reset to 0 BEFORE the Print statement reads it.
    The log always shows "after 0 bars" even after a 10-bar pause.
