2. WHY Mm_ATM.cs TRADES MORE (Root Cause Analysis)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2A. FILTER STACKING IN v3 — THE PRIMARY CAUSE
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  v1 has 4 filters. v3 has 14. Each filter is a MULTIPLICATIVE penalty.
  Most penalties do not have individual floors — they stack freely until the
  final 45%-of-raw floor at the end.

  EXAMPLE: MomentumVWAP raw score = 80 (strong signal)

  v3 filter passes (worst case, lunch + HTF + VP layers all active):
    After Filter  1 (Volume OK):            80.0  (no penalty)
    After Filter  2 (Conflict OK):          80.0
    After Filter  3 (Momentum Exhaust OK):  80.0
    After Filter  4 (Bar Quality OK):       80.0
    After Filter  5 (Night OK):             80.0
    After Filter  6 (HTF bearish -40%):     48.0  **** biggest single killer
    After Filter  7 (VWAP dist 2.5x ATR):   44.4  (penalty 0.925)
    After Filter  8 (Swing bearish -30%):   31.1
    After Filter  9 (Mid-range VP -20%):    24.9
    After Filter 10 (Near POC -15%):        21.1
    After Filter 13 (POC Magnetic -25%):    15.8
    After ApplySmartFilters lunch -15%:     13.4  (0.85x)
    After CalculateSignals -10 pts lunch:    3.4  <-- DOUBLE LUNCH PENALTY
    Floor check: max(3.4, 80*0.45) = 36.0  <- floor saves it

    BUT: the 45% floor is applied inside ApplySmartFilters, BEFORE the -10
    in CalculateSignals. So:
      Floor applied IN ApplySmartFilters: max(13.4, 80*0.45) = 36.0
      Then CalculateSignals subtracts 10:  26.0  <-- BELOW 55 threshold!

  RESULT: An 80-point raw Momentum signal fires in v1 (raw 80 >= threshold 68)
  but may be reduced to 26 in v3 — BELOW the 55 threshold.

2B. AUTO-SELECT STRATEGY vs FIXED STRATEGY
───────────────────────────────────────────
  v1 default = Strategy 0 (Momentum+VWAP). This is the broadest, most
  frequently-triggering strategy. It signals every bar that price crosses
  or holds above/below VWAP with EMA alignment.

  v3 default = Auto Select (Strategy 4). Auto Select:
    (a) Requires 3 consecutive bars identifying the same "best" strategy
        before allowing a switch. If NQ flips conditions (common), the
        strategy lags behind reality and misses entries.
    (b) Applies session overrides:
        - 9:30-10:00 AM: forces Strategy 2 (Liquidity Sweep Reversal).
          LSR requires a PRIOR sweep spike + snap-back. In a trending
          morning open this rarely fires. Hours lost.
        - 11:30-14:00 PM: forces Strategy 0 (Momentum+VWAP).
          But then DOUBLE penalizes scores by -10 (lunch).

2C. HTF EMA FILTER — BIGGEST OVER-FILTER
──────────────────────────────────────────
  Filter 6: if price is 0.5 ATR above/below the HTF EMA, the opposing
  direction gets 0.6x (40% penalty). NQ intraday moves are often 2-4x ATR
  from the daily EMA. This filter fires CONSTANTLY during trending days,
  suppressing all counter-trend signals.

  PROBLEM: The HTF EMA threshold (0.5 ATR) is too tight. A $50-point
  intraday NQ trend will always have price >0.5 ATR from a 200-period HTF EMA.
  This means the BEST intraday trend-following signals (Momentum+VWAP in a
  strong trend) get killed by the HTF filter because price is extended.

2D. DOUBLE LUNCH PENALTY
─────────────────────────
  v3 applies lunch penalty TWICE:
    1. ApplySmartFilters Filter 14: multiply by 0.85 (15% cut)
    2. CalculateSignals: subtract 10 points
  These are not additive — they stack, effectively crushing scores.
  After the 45% floor (in ApplySmartFilters), the final -10 can push
  scores below the threshold that the floor was supposed to protect.

  FIX: Remove the -10 subtraction in CalculateSignals (lines 2591-2597).
  The 0.85x in ApplySmartFilters is sufficient and correct.

2E. POC MAGNETIC FILTER — DOUBLE VP PENALTY
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Filter 10 (near POC: 0.85x) and Filter 13 (POC Magnetic: 0.75x) BOTH
  apply when price is near the POC level. Combined: 0.75 x 0.85 = 0.64.
  This 36% combined cut near POC means the most commonly traded price zone
  (value area) consistently blocks entries.

  FIX: Remove Filter 13 (POC Magnetic) or merge it with Filter 10.
  The two filters represent the same condition.

2F. ENTRY DELAY DIFFERENCE (MINOR)
────────────────────────────────────
  v1: 5 second cooldown. v3: 1 second. v3 is MORE aggressive on re-entry,
  not less. This difference does NOT explain under-trading — it is benign.

3. CONFIDENCE SCORE CALCULATION — DETAILED BREAKDOWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Both versions share the same 4 base strategies with identical raw scoring:

  STRATEGY 0: Momentum + VWAP (max raw score ~110)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
    +30  EMA fast > EMA slow (trend aligned)
    +35  Price crosses above VWAP this bar (primary signal)
    +20  Price above VWAP (continuation — if no cross)
    +20  RSI 50-75 (momentum confirmed, not overbought)
    +15  Price breaks above prior bar's High (momentum bar)
    +10  VWAP cross + bullish bar (conviction bonus)
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    MAX: 110 (cross + conviction + all conditions met)
    TYPICAL: 65-85 when conditions partially met

  STRATEGY 1: Key Level Breakout (max raw score ~100)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    +50  Price crosses above 20-bar high with bullish close
    +25  Price crosses above 20-bar high (weak conviction)
    +25  Price above 20-bar high (continuation)
    +30  ATR-confirmed extension (price moved >15% of ATR above breakout)
    +20  EMA aligned
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    MAX: 100 (conviction cross + ATR confirm + EMA)
    NOTE: +50 requires bullish bar (BOTH cross + close>open). Missing one
    drops to +25, losing 25 raw points immediately.

  STRATEGY 2: Liquidity Sweep Reversal (max raw score ~110)
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    +45  Prior bar swept swing low + current bar snapped back
    +25  Recent sweep (within 5 bars) during continuation
    +30  RSI < 40 (oversold reversal confirmation)
    +25  Snap-back move >30% of ATR
    +10  Volume spike confirms liquidity grab
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    MAX: 110 (fresh sweep + RSI + snap + volume)
    NOTE: Very setup-specific. Raw score is often 0 or 45+25=70.

  STRATEGY 3: Opening Range Breakout (max raw score ~100)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    +55  ORB high cross with bullish close (conviction breakout)
    +30  ORB high cross (no conviction bonus)
    +30  Price above ORB high (continuation)
    +25  EMA aligned
    +20  ORB quality bonus (ATR/ORB range — scales by ORB tightness)
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    MAX: 100 (conviction + EMA + quality)
    NOTE: Only active 9:45 AM - 11:30 AM ET. First signal of day only.

  SMART FILTERS IMPACT ON SCORES:

    v1 (4 filters) — Applied once per strategy call:
      Filter 1: Volume         0.65x - 1.10x  (range: thin to strong)
      Filter 2: Conflict       0.56x - 0.99x  (ratio > 0.65 triggers)
      Filter 3: Exhaustion     up to -45% penalty (near 20-bar extreme + extended)
      Filter 4: Bar Quality    variable (wick % of bar range)
      Max cumulative reduction:  ~37% of raw (typical)
      Max cumulative reduction:  ~60% (worst case)

    v3 (14 filters) — Applied once per CalculateSignals call:
      Filters 1-4:  Same as v1 (slightly recalibrated thresholds)
      Filter  5:  Night Penalty          0.70x (outside 8 AM - 6 PM)
      Filter  6:  HTF Trend              0.60x (vs HTF EMA)  *** most impactful
      Filter  7:  VWAP Distance          0.50x-1.0x (extended from VWAP)
      Filter  8:  Swing Structure        0.70x (HH/HL count bearish)
      Filter  9:  VP Key Level Mid       0.80x (price in mid value area)
      Filter 10:  Near POC               0.85x
      Filter 11:  RSI/Price Divergence   +10 pts bonus (mild)
      Filter 12:  VWAP Slope             +10/-5 pts (mild)
      Filter 13:  POC Magnetic           0.75x (within 1 ATR of POC)
      Filter 14:  Lunch Doldrums         0.85x (11:30-14:00)
      Plus: -10 pts in CalculateSignals during lunch

      Max cumulative reduction: can reach 85%+ of raw score in bad combos

4. TRAIL MECHANICS COMPARISON
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4A. TRAIL DISTANCE FORMULA (SHARED BASE — IDENTICAL IN BOTH)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Both versions use the EXACT same 5-factor trend score:

    Factor 1 (ATR Expansion)    weight 25%  — expanding ATR = trending
    Factor 2 (EMA Spread)       weight 25%  — wider EMA gap = stronger trend
    Factor 3 (Directional Bars) weight 25%  — consecutive bars in trade dir
    Factor 4 (RSI Extremity)    weight 10%  — RSI away from 50 = directional
    Factor 5 (Range Compression) weight 15% — bar range vs ATR

    trailTrendScore in [0.0, 1.0]
    finalDist = lerp(trailMin, trailMax, score) * 0.6 + atrDist * 0.4
    chopMultiplier: score < 0.5 -> 0.5 + score (ramps from 0.5 to 1.0)

  net distance: trailMinPoints (chop) to trailMaxPoints (trend), ATR-scaled

4B. KEY TRAIL DIFFERENCES: v3 ADDS/CHANGES
────────────────────────────────────────────

  CHANGE 1: Dynamic Activation (v3 only)
    v1: trailActive fires at profitPoints >= trailActivationPoints (fixed 8 pts)
    v3: dynamicActivation = max(trailActivationPoints, atrPts * 0.40)

    IMPACT: In a high-ATR environment (ATR=25pts typical for NQ), atrPts = 25.
    dynamicActivation = max(8, 10) = 10 pts. Tier thresholds:
      T1-BE:     1.5x * 10 = 15 pts (v1 needed 2×8 = 16 — similar)
      T2-Strong: 2.5x * 10 = 25 pts (v1 needed 2.5×8 = 20 — HARDER to reach)
      T3-Runner: 4.0x * 10 = 40 pts (v1 needed 4×8 = 32 — MUCH HARDER)

    PROBLEM: With higher ATR, v3 requires more profit to engage each tier.
    T3 at 40 pts is rarely reached intraday. This means fewer "runner" exits
    and more trades stopping out at T1 or T2 level.

  CHANGE 2: Volatility Spike Guard (v3 only)
    if (volatilitySpikeGuardBars > 0 && barRange > atr * 3.0) return;

    IMPACT: During a volatile gap or spike, MonitorAdaptiveTrail returns
    EARLY — the trail does NOT update. If price spikes against the position,
    the trail (and hiddenSL) are both frozen. This can cause large losses
    during gap/spike scenarios. The guard is well-intentioned but removes
    the adaptive trail's ability to protect during the most dangerous bars.

  CHANGE 3: Volatility Spike Widening (v3 CalculateTrailDistance)
    if (atr > atrAvg * TRAIL_SPIKE_MULT) spikeMult = 1.5

    IMPACT: During volatility spikes (ATR expanding), the trail WIDENS by 50%.
    Combined with Change 2 (trail frozen during spike), this means: when the
    spike subsides and trail resumes, it starts from a 1.5x wider position.
    This gives up MORE profit than necessary after a volatile bar, opposite
    of the desired behavior.

    FIX: Remove or reverse spikeMult. During spikes, trail should be TIGHTER
    (use spikeMult = 0.8) to lock in gains before the next counter-move.

  CHANGE 4: Profit Tier Floors (v3 slightly loosened)
    Tier       v1 floor   v3 floor   v3 activation req
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
    T1-BE      2.0x       1.5x       at BE earlier (improvement)
    T2-Strong  45%        40%        2.5x (same but -5% floor: slightly worse)
    T3-Runner  55%        50-55%     4x (same, conditional on trend score)

    NOTE: v3 T2 floor of 40% gives back slightly more than v1's 45%.

  CHANGE 5: Structural Trail Snap (v3 only)
    Scans 5 prior bars for nearest low (long) or high (short). If that
    structural level is tighter than the calculated trail AND still >= trailMin,
    uses structural level instead.

    VERDICT: GOOD addition. Trails structures naturally without arbitrary formula.

  CHANGE 6: Bid/Ask for Trail Trigger (v3 only)
    v3: uses GetCurrentBid()/GetCurrentAsk() in Realtime for trail triggers.
    v1: uses Close[0] always.

    VERDICT: GOOD improvement. Reduces slippage on trail exits.

  CHANGE 7: Trap Tightening of Trail (v3 only)
    if (trapDetected && trapSuspendBarsLeft <= 0) trailDist *= 0.60

    VERDICT: GOOD. Post-entry trap detection tightens trail to protect gains.

4C. SMART TRAIL PAUSE (S-TRAIL) — REVIEW
──────────────────────────────────────────

  PURPOSE: Pauses trail advancement when a MM stop-hunt pullback is
  detected (price pulls back on low volume, EMA still intact) so the
  trail doesn't prematurely exit a winning trade.

  IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW:

    Pause Conditions (ALL must be true):
      1. trailMaxProfitPts >= dynamicActivation * 2 (need 2x activation in profit)
      2. CurrentBar >= 3
      3. Pullback: (High[1] - Close[0]) > atr * 0.3 AND Close[0] < High[1]
      4. EMA still aligned with trade direction
      5. Volume[0] <= avgVol * 1.5 (low volume pullback)
      6. NOT near major S/R (within 1 ATR of 20-bar swing)

    Resume Conditions (any one):
      1. smartTrailPauseBars >= smartTrailMaxPauseBars (timeout)
      2. Price making new HH/LL for 2 consecutive bars (momentum resumes)
      3. Reversal bar after sweep (price recovers from the hunt)

  BUG IDENTIFIED - Resume log:
    The Print statement logs smartTrailPauseBars AFTER resetting it to 0:
      smartTrailPaused = false;
      smartTrailPauseBars = 0;               // <-- reset here
      trailPrice = smartTrailFrozenPrice;
      Print("... after " + smartTrailPauseBars + " bars");  // prints 0 always!

    FIX: Save bars count before reset:
      int pausedFor = smartTrailPauseBars;
      smartTrailPaused = false;
      smartTrailPauseBars = 0;
      trailPrice = smartTrailFrozenPrice;
      Print("... after " + pausedFor + " bars");

  BUG IDENTIFIED - Trail restore on resume:
    On resume, trailPrice is set to smartTrailFrozenPrice (the frozen value).
    If price has MOVED AWAY from the trail while paused (trade in profit),
    restoring the frozen value may set trail LOWER than it should be.
    The existing ratchet logic then catches up on the next tick. This is
    mostly fine, but can cause a 1-tick window where trail is stale.

  POSSIBLE REASON NOT WORKING:
    Condition 1 requires trailMaxProfitPts >= dynamicActivation * 2.
    With dynamic activation = max(8, atrPts * 0.4):
      - NQ ATR = 25 pts typical -> atrPts = 25 -> dynActivation = 10
      - Need: maxProfit >= 20 pts to even EVALUATE for pause
    Most small trades never reach 20 pts profit, so the pause never fires.

    RECOMMENDATION: Change condition to >= dynamicActivation * 1.5 (same
    as T1-BE threshold), allowing pause after breakeven is secured.

  NOT WORKING LIKELY REASON:
    For S-TRAIL to visibly pause the trail, all 6 conditions must align.
    In NQ's fast market, by the time conditions 1-6 all agree, the pullback
    is often already over. The trail guard effectively never fires.

5. SMART SL (SMART STOP LOSS) REVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  MonitorSmartSL() has 3 modes:

  MODE A: Break-Even Lock
  ──────────────────────
    Trigger: profitPts >= tpPoints * (smartSlBePct / 100.0)
    Action:  Move SL to entry + 1 tick
    STATUS:  OK — this works correctly.

  MODE B: Tighten on Trap
  ───────────────────────
    Trigger: !smartSlTightened && trapDetected && trapScore >= 65 && profitPts > 0
    Action:  Move SL to entry - (originalSlDist * 0.75)
    STATUS:  GOOD logic, but trapScore threshold of 65 is high. In practice
    TrapDetector rarely reaches 65 without an extreme adverse move. Consider
    lowering to 50.

  MODE C: LOOSEN on High Confidence
  ───────────────────────────────────
    Trigger: !smartSlBreakevenDone && !smartSlLoosened
             && smartSlEntryConfidence >= minSignalConfidence + 15
    Action:  Extend SL by extendPts
    BUG:     extendPts = Math.Min(10, slPoints + 30 - slPoints)
             = Math.Min(10, 30) = 10 regardless of slPoints.
             The subtraction cancels: slPoints + 30 - slPoints = 30.
             Developer likely intended: Math.Min(10, slPoints * 0.3) or
             something proportional. Always extends by exactly 10 pts.

    FIX:     double extendPts = Math.Min(slPoints * 0.3, 15);
             // Extend by up to 30% of current SL distance, max 15 pts

  NOT WORKING LIKELY REASON:
    smartSlEntryConfidence is only assigned during ExecuteLongEntry /
    ExecuteShortEntry (lines 1793, 1846). If a trade is entered and
    confidence was already penalized to near-threshold, the +15 requirement
    means Mode C almost never triggers. Additionally, since v3 rarely fires
    auto entries (due to over-filtering), Smart SL has fewer chances to run.

6. AUTO-SELECT STRATEGY (Strategy 4) ANALYSIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  v1 AUTO SELECT — The Simpler, Better Version
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
    Logic: Run all 4 strategies each bar. Apply ApplySmartFilters() after
    EACH strategy individually. Pick the one with highest confidence.
    No session override, no stability filter.

    STRENGTH: Each strategy gets filtered independently. If MomentumVWAP
    scores 70 and KLB scores 30 after filtering, picks Momentum. Clean.

  v3 AUTO SELECT — Adds Complexity, Reduces Signals
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Logic: Run all 4 strategies. Pick best raw score. Apply stability filter
    (require 3 consistent bars). Apply session override (hard-coded).
    Apply ApplySmartFilters() ONCE on the winner. Apply lunch -10.

    WEAKNESS 1: Stability filter lags. If MomentumVWAP is best today but
    the 3-bar check hasn't confirmed, bestAutoStrategy might be stuck on
    a stale strategy from yesterday. Result: wrong strategy used.

    WEAKNESS 2: Session override ignores actual market conditions.
    "Force LSR at open" means: if open is a gap-and-go trend day, LSR
    scores low (needs an actual sweep), so no entry is generated despite
    obvious trend momentum.

    WEAKNESS 3: ApplySmartFilters runs ONCE on winner, not per-strategy.
    v1 applied filters per strategy which gave a more accurate "filtered
    best" selection. v3 picks winner on raw score, then filters once.
    The winner on raw score may not be the winner after filtering.

  v1 vs v3 AUTO-SELECT EXAMPLE:
    Raw scores bar X:  MomentumVWAP=80, KeyLevel=50, LSR=30, ORB=0
    v1: Applies filters to each -> filtered: Momentum=65, KL=35, LSR=25, ORB=0
        Picks Momentum=65. Fires if >= 68. -> ENTERS
    v3: Picks Momentum on raw (80). Stability requires 3 bars with Momentum best.
        If last 2 bars had different best, no entry this bar. -> NO ENTRY

7. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR v3 IMPROVEMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIORITY 1 — SIGNAL GENERATION (Most Impact on Trade Frequency)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  R1.1: FIX DOUBLE LUNCH PENALTY
    Remove the -10 pts subtraction in CalculateSignals (lines ~2591-2597).
    The 0.85x in ApplySmartFilters Filter 14 is sufficient.

  R1.2: FIX HTF FILTER THRESHOLD
    Change: dist > 0.5 -> dist > 1.5 (require 1.5 ATR from HTF EMA)
    Currently 0.5 ATR fires constantly during any intraday trend.
    A threshold of 1.5 ATR is more meaningful for HTF structural conflict.

  R1.3: MERGE DUPLICATE POC FILTERS
    Filters 10 and 13 both suppress near-POC signals. Combined 0.64x.
    Remove Filter 13 (POC Magnetic). Filter 10 handles the same condition.

  R1.4: FIX AUTO-SELECT TO USE PER-STRATEGY FILTERING (v1 approach)
    In CalculateSignals when autoStrategy == 4:
    - Run each strategy, apply ApplySmartFilters after each, pick best filtered.
    - Remove the stability (3-bar lock) filter — it delays valid entries.
    - Keep session overrides but allow override to be disabled by parameter.

  R1.5: RELAX SESSION OVERRIDE OR ADD PARAMETER
    Add: bool enableSessionOverride = true (default) / false
    The forced LSR at open blocks momentum-day entries at the most
    profitable time of day. Make it optional.

  R1.6: CHANGE DEFAULT autoStrategy TO 0
    While developing/testing, default to Strategy 0 (Momentum+VWAP).
    This matches v1 and gives predictable, well-understood behavior.
    Auto Select can be enabled explicitly when ready.

PRIORITY 2 — TRAIL IMPROVEMENTS
──────────────────────────────────

  R2.1: REMOVE VOLATILITY SPIKE WIDENING (OR REVERSE IT)
    Change: if (atr > atrAvg * TRAIL_SPIKE_MULT) spikeMult = 1.5
    To:     if (atr > atrAvg * TRAIL_SPIKE_MULT) spikeMult = 0.85
    During volatility spikes, TIGHTEN trail to lock gains.

  R2.2: VOLATILITY SPIKE GUARD — ALLOW TRAIL TO TIGHTEN, NOT FREEZE
    Instead of returning early when barRange > atr*3, allow trail to
    move to tightest safe position (min distance). Don't freeze entirely.

  R2.3: SMART TRAIL PAUSE THRESHOLD
    Change pause condition: trailMaxProfitPts >= dynamicActivation * 2
    To:                     trailMaxProfitPts >= dynamicActivation * 1.5
    This allows pause to protect T1-BE trades, not just T2+.

  R2.4: FIX S-TRAIL RESUME LOG BUG
    Save pausedFor before resetting smartTrailPauseBars, then print it.

PRIORITY 3 — SMART SL FIXES
──────────────────────────────

  R3.1: FIX SMART SL MODE C EXTENSION FORMULA
    Change: double extendPts = Math.Min(10, slPoints + 30 - slPoints);
    To:     double extendPts = Math.Min(15.0, slPoints * 0.30);
    This makes extension proportional to the trade's SL distance.

  R3.2: LOWER TRAP TIGHTEN THRESHOLD
    Change: trapScore >= 65
    To:     trapScore >= 50
    65 is rarely reached until the position is already significantly
    adverse. 50 triggers earlier while the trade still has room.

  R3.3: VERIFY smartSlEvalBars IS NEEDED
    The field increments each tick but is not used in any condition.
    If intended for "evaluate after N bars" logic, add the check.
    If unused, remove to reduce confusion.

PRIORITY 4 — GENERAL QUALITY
──────────────────────────────

  R4.1: ADD DIAGNOSTIC LOGGING FOR FILTER PASSES/FAILS
    Log which filters triggered and their multipliers when autoMode && bar%50==0.
    This enables tuning without live-trading guesses.

  R4.2: EXPOSE FILTER WEIGHTS AS PARAMETERS
    HTF threshold, VWAP distance penalty start, conflict ratio, exhaustion
    threshold — all hard-coded. Making them parameters allows backtesting
    different calibrations without code changes.

  R4.3: DEFAULT autoStrategy BACK TO 0 FOR TESTING
    Set default to 0, not 4. Auto Select (4) is too complex to debug.
    Test each strategy independently, confirm they fire, then enable Auto.

8. SUMMARY: WHY Mm_ATM.cs OUTPERFORMS Mm_ATM_v3.cs
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. FEWER FILTERS: v1 has 4 focused filters vs v3's 14 stacking filters.
     The 10 additional filters in v3 have compounding multiplicative effect
     that crushes signals below threshold even when the market is clearly
     trending.

  2. SIMPLER DEFAULT CONFIG: v1 default = strategy 0 (constant signals),
     v3 default = auto-select (complex, delayed, session-overridden).

  3. CLEAN LUNCH HANDLING: v1 has no lunch penalty. v3 double-penalizes lunch.

  4. BETTER TRAIL BEHAVIOR: v1 trail is clean — no spike guard, no spike
     widening. v3 freezes trail during exactly the bars it most needs updating.

  5. SIGNAL THRESHOLD CALIBRATED TO FILTER SET: v1 uses 68% threshold with
     4 filters (appropriate). v3 uses 55% threshold after 14 filters
     (threshold was lowered to compensate for over-filtering, but 55% after
     14 stacked filters still produces fewer entries than 68% after 4 filters).

  THE CORE INSIGHT:
  ─────────────────
  v3 was designed to "only take the best trades" but the filter stack is
  so aggressive that it eliminates most good trades along with bad ones.
  v1's simpler filter set allows more signal through at a higher threshold,
  producing more trades with comparable quality.

  The v3 features (Smart Trail, Smart SL, HTF filter, VP filter) are
  individually sound ideas, but their combined effect reduces the strategy
  to near-zero entries. The path forward is to:
    (a) Remove duplicate/overlapping filters (POC Magnetic overlaps Near POC)
    (b) Relax the HTF threshold from 0.5 to 1.5 ATR
    (c) Fix the double lunch penalty
    (d) Return Auto Select to the v1 per-strategy filtering approach
    (e) Keep the good v3 trail additions (structural snap, bid/ask triggers,
        trap tightening) but remove spike widening and fix spike guard
